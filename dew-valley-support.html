Dew Valley

Support

Questions, issues, or feedback: here's how to get help.

How does Dew Valley work?

Log the water you drink each day with the + button. It fills your Well, and that water is the energy your farm runs on: chopping, tilling and watering each use some. Drink, farm, grow!

I bought something but didn't get it

Purchases are added as soon as Apple confirms them. If one didn't arrive, close and reopen the app: unfinished purchases are picked up again on launch. Still missing? Email us with the date and what you bought.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Will my farm carry over to a new phone?

Your farm is saved on your device. It carries over when you restore your new iPhone from a backup (iCloud or computer) of your old one.

Is Dew Valley medical advice?

No. Dew Valley is a game to help you build a drinking habit. Talk to a doctor about how much water is right for you.

I found a bug, or something isn't working

Please email us with as much detail as you can: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.

Deleting your data

Everything lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
