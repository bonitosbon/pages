Dewfield

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-28

Dewfield is a cozy farming game where the water you drink in real life powers your farm. This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Dewfield does not collect, share or sell any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What's saved on your device

The water you log, your daily goal and streak, your farm, items, decorations, coins, gems and settings. They are saved only on your iPhone, are never sent to us or anyone else, and are deleted when you delete the app.

No accounts, no tracking

There's no sign-up or login. Dewfield has no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details: the app only gets Apple's confirmation so it can add what you bought.

Sharing pictures

Share cards are made on your device and are only shared when you choose to, through the iOS share sheet, to the app you pick. Saving a picture asks your permission to add it to Photos; the app can't read your photos.

Health information

The water you log is only used inside the game. Dewfield is not a medical app and doesn't give medical advice.

Children

Dewfield doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
