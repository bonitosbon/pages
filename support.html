Support

Questions, issues, or feedback — here's how to get help.

How do I get Preppo Pro back after reinstalling or switching devices?

Open the Preppo Pro screen (you'll see it if you try to create more than 2 trips) and tap Restore Purchases. Your lifetime purchase is tied to your App Store or Google Play account, not your device, so it restores automatically once you're signed in with the same account you originally purchased with.

Does Preppo Pro auto-renew or charge me again?

No. Preppo Pro is a one-time purchase — you pay once and keep it forever. There's no subscription, no recurring billing, and nothing to cancel.

How does Party Packing work?

Start a trip in "Party Pack" mode to get an invite code, or join someone else's trip with a code they share. Everyone on a shared trip can add items, claim bags, and see packing progress update in real time — Party Packing is completely free for every participant, including people who join a trip someone else created.

My scan didn't recognize an item, or got it wrong

Preppo scans on-device first, with a cloud fallback for trickier photos. On-device recognition is intentionally cautious — if it's not confident, it won't guess. You can always add or rename items manually if a scan doesn't come out right.

I found a bug, or something isn't working

Please email us with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.

Deleting your data

Most of your data lives only on your own device — deleting the app removes it. If you've used Party Packing, you can leave or delete a shared trip from within the app at any time. For anything else, contact us below and we'll help.
