Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-19

Preppo is a travel packing app. This page explains what information the app handles and why.

No account required

Preppo doesn't ask for an email address or password. Your trips, packing lists, and settings are stored on your own device. Nothing is created on our servers unless you choose to use Party Packing.

Party Packing (optional)

If you start or join a shared trip with other people, the app creates an anonymous identity for your device (no name, email, or phone number attached) so the shared trip can sync between everyone's phones in real time. The trip's destination, dates, packing list, and the display name/avatar you choose for that trip are stored so other invited members can see them. Deleting a shared trip (as the organizer) or leaving it (as a member) removes your access to that shared data.

Camera

If you use the camera to scan items into your packing list, photos are analyzed on your device first using a bundled on-device model — most scans never leave your phone. If the on-device scan can't confidently identify an item, the photo is automatically sent to Google's Gemini API as a fallback for a second look. We don't store the photo ourselves; how Google handles data sent to its API is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Location

If you allow location access, it's used to note where you last saw a packed item (e.g. "left in the hotel room") and, only when needed, to look up the name of that place. This is usually answered from data cached on your device. If nothing is cached for that spot, your coordinates are sent to a mapping lookup service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, or your device's own built-in location service) to get a place name back. This is never sent to us or to other party members, even on a shared trip.

Other services this app talks to

To provide weather forecasts, area suggestions, currency conversion, and location lookups, the app sends non-identifying requests (like a country name or coordinates) to these third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo (weather forecasts)
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass for nearby places, Nominatim for place-name lookups)
  • A city/population lookup service (suggesting areas within a country)
  • Frankfurter (currency exchange rates)
  • Google Gemini (fallback photo recognition, only when on-device scanning can't identify an item)

None of these receive your name, contact details, or device identity.

Children's privacy

Preppo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
